# UAE Family Trip: Dubai + Abu Dhabi, 12–18 Dec 2026

## Trip summary

- **Travellers:** 4 adults (me, sister, mother 60+, father 60+). Indian passports.
- **Dates:** Sat 12 Dec – Fri 18 Dec 2026 (6 nights).
- **Route:** Delhi → Dubai (DXB). Dubai 5 nights. Abu Dhabi 1 night. Abu Dhabi (AUH) → Delhi.
- **Booking:** Indian operator package (flights, visa, hotels, private transfers, tours).
- **Pace:** Relaxed. One big activity and one light activity per day. Afternoon rest at the hotel.
- **Food:** Vegetarian, eggs OK, onion and garlic OK.
- **Hotels:** Dubai Downtown or Business Bay (4-star). Abu Dhabi Yas Island (4-star).

> **Caveat:** This plan was made without internet access. Opening hours, ticket prices, visa rules and event dates come from general knowledge and may have changed. Verify each item with the operator or official site before you pay.

## Why this routing

- Flying into Dubai and out of Abu Dhabi means only one hotel change and no drive back to Dubai.
- The BAPS Mandir is on the Dubai–Abu Dhabi highway, so you visit it on the way.
- A Yas Island hotel is a short walk from Ferrari World and about 15 minutes from AUH airport.
- Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque is closed to visitors on Friday mornings, so the mosque visit is on Thursday.

## Day-by-day plan

### Day 1 — Sat 12 Dec: Arrive in Dubai

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| ~08:00 | Fly Delhi → Dubai (about 3.5 h). Dubai is 1.5 h behind India. |
| ~10:30 | Land at DXB. Request wheelchair assistance for parents (free, request at booking). |
| 12:00 | Private transfer to the Downtown hotel. Ask for early check-in. |
| 12:30–16:00 | Lunch, then rest. |
| 17:30–21:00 | Dubai Mall walk, Dubai Fountain show (every 30 min after sunset), dinner at the mall. |

**Walking:** Light. Mall has seating and wheelchairs for rent.

### Day 2 — Sun 13 Dec: Old Dubai and Burj Khalifa

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 09:00–13:00 | Half-day Old Dubai tour with guide and car: Al Fahidi historical area, Dubai Museum area, abra boat across the Creek (AED 1–2), spice souk, gold souk. Photo stops at Jumeirah Mosque and Burj Al Arab. |
| 13:00–14:00 | Vegetarian lunch in Bur Dubai or Karama (many Indian veg restaurants, for example Saravana Bhavan or a Rajdhani-style thali place). |
| 14:00–16:00 | Rest at the hotel. |
| 16:30–18:00 | Burj Khalifa, Level 124/125, sunset slot (sunset is about 17:30). |
| 18:00–20:30 | Fountain from the waterfront, dinner. |

**Walking:** Moderate at the souks. The guide can keep the souk walk short and let parents sit at a café.

### Day 3 — Mon 14 Dec: Miracle Garden and desert safari

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 09:00–11:30 | Dubai Miracle Garden. Flat paths, best in the cooler morning. |
| 12:00–15:00 | Lunch and rest. |
| 15:00–21:00 | Desert safari with BBQ dinner (ask for a veg menu). |

**Safari for parents:** Book a "no dune bashing" option, or a separate 4x4 that drives straight to the camp. Siblings can do dune bashing in a different car. At the camp: camel ride (optional), henna, tanoura and fire shows, veg buffet.

**Carry:** Jackets. The desert gets cold (about 15°C) after sunset.

### Day 4 — Tue 15 Dec: Museum of the Future, Dubai Frame, Global Village

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 10:00–12:00 | Museum of the Future (timed tickets, book weeks ahead). Fully indoor, lifts available. |
| 12:15–13:15 | Dubai Frame (10 min drive). Lift to the top, glass floor bridge. |
| 13:30–16:30 | Lunch and rest. |
| 17:00–21:30 | Global Village. Country pavilions, shopping, street food (many Indian and veg stalls). Rent wheelchairs for parents at the entrance. |

**Walking:** Global Village covers a large area. Pick 4–5 pavilions, not all of them.

### Day 5 — Wed 16 Dec: Palm, JBR beach, dhow cruise

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 09:30–11:00 | Optional: Hindu Temple, Jebel Ali (check if online slot booking is still needed). |
| 11:00–12:30 | Palm Jumeirah: monorail ride, Atlantis from outside. Optional: The View at The Palm. |
| 12:30–14:30 | JBR beach walk and lunch at The Beach / The Walk. |
| 14:30–18:30 | Rest at the hotel. Optional shopping for siblings (Dubai Shopping Festival offers). |
| 20:00–22:00 | Dubai Marina dhow dinner cruise (seated, veg buffet). |

### Day 6 — Thu 17 Dec: Drive to Abu Dhabi, BAPS Mandir, Ferrari World, Grand Mosque

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 08:30 | Check out. Private car to Abu Dhabi with all luggage. |
| 09:45–12:00 | BAPS Hindu Mandir, Abu Mreikha (about 1 h from Dubai). Free entry, but register online in advance. |
| 12:00–13:30 | Drive to Yas Island (about 45 min). Lunch at Yas Mall. |
| 14:00 | Check in at the Yas Island hotel. |
| 14:30–17:30 | **Split:** Siblings go to Ferrari World. Parents rest at the hotel. |
| 18:00–19:45 | All four: Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque at night (about 25 min from Yas). The mosque is lit after sunset. |
| 20:00 | Dinner, back to the hotel. |

**Dress code (Mandir and mosque):** Shoulders and knees covered, no tight or see-through clothes. Women must cover their hair at the mosque, so carry a scarf. Carry passports or Emirates IDs for mosque entry.

### Day 7 — Fri 18 Dec: Louvre and fly home

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 10:00 | Check out. Luggage goes in the car (or ask for late checkout). |
| 10:30–13:00 | Louvre Abu Dhabi, Saadiyat Island (20 min). Benches throughout; wheelchairs available. |
| 13:00–14:30 | Lunch. |
| 14:30–16:00 | Yas Mall: last-minute shopping, or rest. |
| 16:30 | Reach AUH airport (15 min from Yas). Request wheelchair assistance. |
| ~19:30 | Fly Abu Dhabi → Delhi (about 3.5 h). Land around 01:30 IST. |

**Note:** If the flight leaves before 18:00, drop Yas Mall and go straight to the airport after lunch.

## Brief for the operator

Send this when asking for quotes:

- 4 adults (2 seniors 60+), 12–18 Dec 2026. Open-jaw: DEL→DXB morning flight, AUH→DEL evening flight.
- 2 rooms. Dubai 5 nights in Downtown or Business Bay (4-star, breakfast included). Abu Dhabi 1 night on Yas Island.
- Private car with driver for all transfers and tours, not shared coach tours.
- Include: Old Dubai half-day tour, Burj Khalifa 124/125 sunset, Miracle Garden, desert safari with no dune bashing for 2 seniors and veg dinner, Museum of the Future, Dubai Frame, Global Village, Marina dhow dinner cruise, Palm monorail, Abu Dhabi transfer with BAPS Mandir and Grand Mosque, Louvre, 2× Ferrari World.
- All meals in the package must be vegetarian (eggs OK).
- Ask for a quote with flights and without flights.

## Packing and health checklist

- [ ] Passports valid until at least June 2027 (6 months after return).
- [ ] Visa copies, hotel vouchers and return ticket (printed and on phone).
- [ ] Light jackets for evenings (15–18°C) and the desert.
- [ ] Modest clothes and a headscarf for the Mandir and mosque.
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes. Sunglasses, sunscreen, cap.
- [ ] Parents' medicines in hand luggage, with prescriptions.
- [ ] International roaming, or buy a UAE tourist SIM / eSIM at the airport.
- [ ] Some AED cash for souks and abra. Cards work almost everywhere else.

## Weather

December in the UAE: about 24–26°C during the day and 15–18°C at night. Mostly sunny, with rare rain. Sunset is about 17:30.
