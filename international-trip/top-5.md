# December 2026 Family Trip: Top 5 Countries

## Requirements

- **Travellers:** 4 adults (me, sister, mother 60+, father 60+). Indian passports, no existing visas.
- **Mobility:** Parents moderate. Short walks fine, avoid many stairs and treks.
- **Food:** Vegetarian, eggs OK.
- **From:** Delhi. Leave on or after 1 Dec 2026, back in India by 26 Dec 2026.
- **Duration:** Up to 7 days (6 nights).
- **Weather:** No uncomfortable heat while walking outdoors. Hot is fine if sightseeing is mostly air-conditioned.
- **Interests:** Nature and mountains, culture and temples, beaches and islands, shopping and city life.
- **Style:** Package / guided tour, maximum 2 cities.
- **Budget:** About INR 1–1.5 lakh per person (not fixed).

## Assumptions and caveats

- Costs are per person, based on 2 shared rooms, 4-star hotels, economy return flights from Delhi, booked about 4 weeks ahead.
- Add INR 1–2k per person for travel insurance (recommended for travellers aged 60+).
- Visa rules come from general knowledge, not live verification. Thailand, Malaysia and Sri Lanka rules change often. Confirm with the embassy or airline before booking.
- Fares are estimates, not live quotes. Prices rise weekly this close to December, so book flights within 1–2 weeks.
- Every passport must be valid for at least 6 months after the return date.
- Trips ending before about 20 Dec avoid Christmas peak pricing.

## 1. Thailand: Bangkok + Phuket (or Krabi)

**Why:** Covers all four interests. No visa needed. December is the dry peak season. Vegetarian food is very easy to find. Temperatures are 28–31°C, but malls, hotels and boats are air-conditioned.

**Major attractions:** Grand Palace, Wat Arun, Safari World, Chao Phraya dinner cruise, Phi Phi and James Bond Island boat trips, Phuket Old Town, Big Buddha. Shopping at Pratunam, MBK and ICONSIAM.

**Dates:** Sat 5 Dec – Fri 11 Dec (6 nights)

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Fly to Bangkok, rest, dinner cruise |
| 2 | Temples in the morning (cooler), malls in the afternoon |
| 3 | Safari World and Marine Park |
| 4 | Fly to Phuket, evening at the beach |
| 5 | Phi Phi Islands speedboat day |
| 6 | Phuket city tour, Big Buddha, Old Town |
| 7 | Fly home |

**Budget per person:** about INR 70–90k

- Flights: INR 25–32k
- Hotels: INR 15–18k
- Tours: INR 10–12k
- Internal flight: INR 5k
- Food: INR 7k

## 2. Vietnam (North): Hanoi + Ha Long Bay

**Why:** Ha Long Bay is one of Asia's most beautiful sights. Hanoi is 15–20°C in December, ideal for walking with parents. This is the best match for "most beautiful and right for the season".

**Major attractions:** Overnight Ha Long Bay cruise (very parent-friendly), Ninh Binh boat ride through karst cliffs, Hanoi Old Quarter, Temple of Literature, Hoan Kiem Lake, water puppet show, night markets.

**Dates:** Sat 12 Dec – Fri 18 Dec (6 nights)

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Fly to Hanoi, evening walk in the Old Quarter |
| 2 | Hanoi city tour and water puppet show |
| 3 | Ninh Binh day trip (boat, no hiking) |
| 4–5 | Ha Long Bay overnight cruise |
| 6 | Back in Hanoi, shopping and rest |
| 7 | Fly home |

**Budget per person:** about INR 65–90k

- Flights: INR 22–30k
- E-visa: INR 2.2k
- Hotels: INR 10–14k
- Cruise: INR 12–15k
- Tours: INR 7k
- Food: INR 6k

**Notes:** The e-visa takes 3–5 working days. Apply in November. Choose a cruise that serves vegetarian meals, and carry snacks.

## 3. UAE: Dubai + Abu Dhabi

**Why:** December is the best season, at 20–26°C. Best shopping of all five options; the Dubai Shopping Festival usually starts in mid-December. Abu Dhabi has the BAPS Hindu Mandir, a major draw for parents. Vegetarian food is everywhere.

**Major attractions:** Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall fountain, desert safari, dhow cruise, Miracle Garden, Global Village, JBR beach. In Abu Dhabi: Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, BAPS Mandir, and optionally the Louvre or Ferrari World.

**Dates:** Sat 12 Dec – Thu 17 Dec (5 nights)

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Arrive, dhow cruise at the Marina |
| 2 | City tour, Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall |
| 3 | Miracle Garden, desert safari in the evening |
| 4 | Drive to Abu Dhabi: Grand Mosque and BAPS Mandir, stay overnight |
| 5 | Back to Dubai: Global Village and shopping |
| 6 | Fly home |

**Budget per person:** about INR 90k–1.2L

- Flights: INR 25–35k
- Visa: INR 7–9k
- Hotels: INR 18–25k
- Tours: INR 15–22k
- Food: INR 8–10k

**Weak spot:** No mountains or real nature, apart from the desert.

## 4. Singapore + Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur)

**Why:** A classic first trip for Indian families. Very clean, air-conditioned everywhere, and lots of vegetarian food (Little India in both cities). Two countries in one trip.

**Major attractions:** Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa, Universal Studios, Night Safari, Marina Bay. In Malaysia: Petronas Towers, Genting Highlands cable car (cool hill station), Batu Caves Murugan temple (272 steps, optional for parents).

**Dates:** Sun 6 Dec – Sat 12 Dec (6 nights: 3 in Singapore, 3 in Kuala Lumpur)

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Arrive in Singapore, Marina Bay light show |
| 2 | Sentosa and Universal Studios |
| 3 | Gardens by the Bay, Night Safari |
| 4 | Bus or flight to Kuala Lumpur, Petronas Towers in the evening |
| 5 | Genting Highlands day trip |
| 6 | Batu Caves, KL city tour, shopping |
| 7 | Fly home |

**Budget per person:** about INR 1.05–1.35L

- Flights (into Singapore, out of KL): INR 30–40k
- Singapore visa: INR 3–4k
- Hotels: INR 25–30k
- Attractions: INR 20–25k
- Food: INR 9k

**Notes:** December is rainy, but most sightseeing is indoors. Singapore hotels are expensive. Malaysia needs no visa. The Singapore visa goes through an agent and takes 3–5 days.

## 5. Sri Lanka: Kandy + Bentota

**Why:** Shortest flight (about 3.5 hours) and the cheapest option. Hills, tea estates, beaches and Ramayana temples (Sita Amman Temple). Food is close to South Indian.

**Major attractions:** Temple of the Tooth, Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage, Nuwara Eliya tea country day trip, Bentota beach, Madu River boat ride, Galle Fort, Colombo shopping.

**Dates:** Sat 12 Dec – Fri 18 Dec (6 nights)

| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Fly to Colombo, drive to Kandy via Pinnawala |
| 2 | Temple of the Tooth and botanical garden |
| 3 | Nuwara Eliya day trip: tea factory, Sita Amman Temple |
| 4 | Drive to Bentota, evening at the beach |
| 5 | Madu River boat ride and Galle Fort |
| 6 | Colombo shopping, back to the beach hotel |
| 7 | Fly home |

**Budget per person:** about INR 65–85k

- Flights: INR 20–28k
- Package (private car and hotels): INR 38–48k
- Food: INR 5k
- Entry tickets: INR 3–5k

**Notes:** Expect some rain in the hill country in December. The west coast is mostly dry.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Bhutan:** Snowy mountains and monasteries, but no beach or shopping.
- **Kazakhstan:** Snow and visa-free entry, but vegetarian food is harder to find.
- **Bali:** December is the rainy season.
